# Contributing to HAF

Thank you for your interest in contributing to the Hani Aviation Framework™.

HAF is an open methodology and contributions are welcome from aviation professionals, researchers, analysts, developers, educators, and other interested contributors.

## Areas of Contribution

You can contribute through:

* Methodology improvements
* Aviation intelligence workflows
* Research techniques
* Safety and quality perspectives
* AI-assisted research practices
* Documentation
* Templates
* Examples
* Editorial review
* Practical use cases

## Contribution Principles

Contributions should be:

* Accurate
* Evidence-based
* Clearly documented
* Respectful of aviation regulations and professional standards
* Transparent about assumptions and limitations
* Free from fabricated sources or unsupported claims

## Aviation Sources

Where factual aviation information is introduced, contributors should identify the relevant source whenever practical.

Authoritative sources should be preferred for regulatory, safety, operational, and technical claims.

## AI-Assisted Contributions

AI tools may be used during development.

However, contributors remain responsible for reviewing and validating submitted content.

AI-generated content must not be submitted as verified fact without appropriate human review.

## Pull Requests

When submitting a pull request:

1. Clearly describe the proposed change.
2. Explain why the change improves HAF.
3. Identify relevant sources where applicable.
4. Keep changes focused.
5. Review the final content for accuracy and clarity.

## Discussion

Constructive criticism is encouraged.

The purpose of HAF is continuous improvement through transparent professional collaboration.
